About Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second-smallest planet in the Solar System. It is often called the "Red Planet" due to its reddish appearance.

  • Distance from Sun: 227.9 million km
  • Day length: 16.5 hours
  • Moons: 2
  • Surface temperature: -125°C to 20°C
