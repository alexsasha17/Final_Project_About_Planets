About Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and the third-largest in the Solar System. It is an ice giant with a blue-green coloration due to methane in its atmosphere.

  • Distance from Sun: 2.87 billion km
  • Day length: 17 hours
  • Moons: 27
  • Surface temperature: -195°C
